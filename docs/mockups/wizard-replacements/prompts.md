## arcane-cosmonaut

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: visual exploration sheet for a replacement player character in Somnonaut, a 2D side-view platformer. This is a polished game art mockup, not implementation or a sprite atlas.
Style/medium: striking minimalist risograph print made of flat filled cut-paper shapes, subtle grain, coarse screened ink, slight plate misregistration. ABSOLUTELY NO OUTLINES, no strokes around silhouettes, no ink line drawing, no 3D, no painted gradients, no realistic rendering. Big simple shapes that could be drawn procedurally. Dark midnight indigo background, restrained federal-blue clothing, pale cream paper knockouts, sparse golden eyes. Spell-specific accents: burgundy for HEX, teal for ASTRAL, orange for AWARENESS. Player dash readiness uses a small concentrated fluorescent pink glow printed as concentric screened filled shapes, not a neon line.
Composition: landscape 3:2 concept sheet with generous clean spacing and a consistent editorial layout. Large full-body three-quarter side-view character at left taking about 40% of the sheet, facing right. Upper right: three clearly separated closeups of the changing spell cue, labeled HEX, ASTRAL, AWARENESS. Middle right: three smaller full-body side-view poses labeled DASH READY, DASH SPENT, DASHING, making the independent dash signal obvious. Bottom: one wide simple side-view gameplay vignette, labeled GAMEPLAY SCALE, dark grotto with blue blocky rock ledges and sparse quiet blue vegetation, player small in the scene, approximately 55 px high in the final sheet. No other characters, enemies or UI.
Typography: sparse clean cream uppercase print labels. One title at top, small labels only, no invented paragraphs.
Constraints: anonymous face, compact readable platformer proportions, planted boots, 3 or 4 dominant silhouette shapes, clothing and accessories bend and trail with movement, never squash or stretch the body. Dash pose has two fading solid silhouette echoes behind the character. Gameplay background much darker and less saturated than character. Readiness glow must be visibly absent in the spent state. No conventional wizard pointed hat, no ornamental border, no watermarks. Every panel shows the same coherent character. Keep the spell cue and dash cue visually separate.
Title (verbatim): "ARCANE COSMONAUT".
Primary request: an arcane cosmonaut with a GLASS HELMET and arcane gadgets that show the carried spell and dash readiness.
Subject: compact blue pressure suit with a short cloth tabard, chunky boots, a large round glass bubble helmet over an anonymous midnight face with two tiny warm eyes. Show the glass as one translucent screened pale-blue filled disc with a broad cream reflection crescent and dark neck gasket, WITHOUT any contour stroke. A small arcane backpack and one large readable forearm spell chamber, simple rounded solid shapes, an occult instrument rather than a technological soldier. Keep gadgets few and bold.
Spell cue: the same forearm gadget contains an interchangeable glowing spell seal, large and visible in closeups: HEX is a burgundy comet/spark; ASTRAL a teal spirit/shroud silhouette; AWARENESS an orange almond with a central pupil. The unit's ink and seal change, the suit stays blue. When casting, the gadget opens a small fan of filled magical shapes.
Dash cue: a small comet-shaped reservoir at the upper backpack/shoulder is brightly pink with a paper-white center when dash is ready; it is a plain dark-blue inert shape when spent. In the DASHING pose it flares backward, helmet remains round, short tabard trails, two pink solid silhouette echoes follow.
In the gameplay vignette ensure the oversized round helmet, forearm seal, and single shoulder reservoir still read. Aim for haunted storybook cosmonaut with practical arcane gear, not detailed science-fiction armor.
```
