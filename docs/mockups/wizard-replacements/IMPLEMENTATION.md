# Character selection checkpoint

F7 → Look → Character offers the original Wizard, Arcane cosmonaut, Mask shaman and The Fool. The Fool is the default (`RisoPrint.DEFAULT_CHARACTER`); the choice lasts through travel and new runs in the current scene. These are playable procedural versions of the concept sheets, using the existing riso print and movement rig.

Implemented:

- Cosmonaut: simple suit, back cape, glass helmet, corked backpack flask containing the spell, pink dash antenna, chest health beads and a separate protection flame.
- Shaman: a different mask silhouette for every spell, floating gloves, crooked staff, a dangling chain of glowing health beads and a protection flame at the staff's crook.
- Fool: a poncho broadest at the shoulders that tapers to a short hem that swings freely when moving, over shaded legs and two short cuffed boots set a little wider apart than the wizard's, pale tufted hair, a short neck above a lighter collar that droops over the shoulders and carries large health beads along its curve (hearts past three hang centred on a second, longer strand: a pendant at four, a pair at five, three at six), a level beret whose long tapered plume shows dash readiness (pink when ready, pale grey once spent), gloves coming out of the poncho (one gripping the bindle pole at the shoulder, one holding the lantern's bail at hip height), a lantern with one plain teardrop flame that, without spawn protection, glows a dim, slowly pulsing pink around an ember, and a knotted cloth bindle. Every spell changes the whole bag's shape, folds, color and arcane mark.
- All three: current/max health (including six hearts with vigor), independent dash and spawn protection cues, spell recharge and running time, Mend draughts, astral translucency and matching dash, ghost and portal silhouettes. Unlit lanterns leave a world-positioned pink smoke trail, fading over 1.4 seconds. Each puff drifts sideways away from the traveler's current position (judged from just in front of them), so smoke never blows back across the body whichever way they face; a centred chest flame's smoke drifts back past the helmet. Collision and level generation are unchanged.
- `RisoCostume.MOTION` sets how strongly each traveler follows the wizard's bob, head lag, lean and landing bow. The Fool takes about a third to a half of each, because its tall, stiff poncho visibly squashed and skewed at the wizard's full strength. The others still use full strength. The Fool's hem, by contrast, swings at most of the springs' strength (`FOOL_HEM_SWAY`), and its widest corners follow part of the swing, so the poncho moves as cloth while the body stays steady.
- The Fool's parts keep their own springs (`RisoCostume.step`, run each physics step after the wizard's rig). The lantern and the bindle bag are pendulums, swung by the body's acceleration, the walk and landings, and lightly damped so they sway on after the Fool stops. They keep their swing through a turn. The bindle pole bounces about its glove, the lantern hand swings with the stride, and the head nods against acceleration on its neck while following most of the body's own head lag.
- Standing still, the Fool breathes, its lantern and bag sway a little, and every few seconds the feather flicks. A turn starts with the Fool's head: the face, hair, beret and feather turn the moment the player turns, mirrored about the head's own centre, and the body follows a few frames later as it swings round (`head_facing`). An earlier version narrowed the whole figure through a turn, which read as a cutout flipping and was dropped. At a wall ahead, the Fool's lantern glove plants on it (shoulder high when pushing, reaching up in turn while climbing) and the lantern leans back from the wall instead of being squashed against it.
- All travelers carry astral as a crescent moon and awareness as an eye with a dark upright pupil, as on the concept sheets. The Fool's live dash echoes match its swinging bag and lantern and its boots; ghosts and portal trips use the same outline at rest.
- `RisoCostume.GAIT` gives each traveler a stride length, step height and stance width. The Fool walks with longer, slower, higher steps (stride 30 against the wizard's 22, lift 3.6 against 1.9). The body's bob follows the same stride, so it stays in step with the feet.
- Fixed blue cloth uses an eighth ink plate, keeping the characters blue in the garden's green palette. The selected spell colors its accessory independently of F7's original-wizard Robe setting.

Visual tuning to review next:

- Readability at the default camera zoom: the Fool's beads are now spaced to stay countable, but the flask glyph and tiny Mend draughts may still need larger shapes after playtesting.
- Walls and climbing have been checked in posed stills only; play against real walls to judge the glove plant, the flattened cloth and the climbing feet.
- The Cosmonaut and Shaman still use the wizard's cloth springs and full motion; they may want their own `MOTION`, `GAIT` and swinging parts like the Fool's.
- Other spells still use the game's ability marks inside the bag, mask and flask.
- Character choice is a session preference. Saving it across app launches would need a separate persistent presentation setting; it is not part of the run save.

Validation: all 44 quick tests passed, including `tests/character_styles_test.gd`, and the rendered `tests/riso_pin_test.gd` passed. `tests/capture_characters.gd` renders the protected, unprotected, moving smoke, spent dash, astral and awareness comparisons, gameplay views and F7 selector into `game-captures/`. `tests/capture_walk.gd` films the default traveler under real physics (standing, starting a run, stopping, jumping and landing) as a contact sheet in `art-captures/walk.png`. These were inspected close up and at gameplay scale. Concept sheets and their prompts are retained here; `.gdignore` excludes these review images from Godot imports.

An intermittent Godot shutdown warning (two ObjectDB objects and one resource still in use) appeared during a character-test run; the subsequent verbose run exited cleanly. If it recurs, inspect test teardown and the background level loader before treating it as a costume leak.
