# Wizard replacement mockups: revision 2

Made with the built-in imagegen tool, editing each original concept sheet. All three use detached floating hands, three beads for base health, an independent warm flame for spawn-lantern protection, and a separate pink dash cue.

## arcane-cosmonaut-v2

Reference: `arcane-cosmonaut.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: revised Somnonaut player character concept sheet.
Input image: the attached prior concept sheet is an EDIT TARGET and visual identity/style reference. Redesign only this same character according to the changes below. Preserve the character identity, dark indigo environment, risograph medium, flat filled shapes, warm faceless eyes where present, compact boots, and the existing spell/dash color system.
Global anatomy change: the character has NO ARMS AT ALL. No upper arms, no forearms, no shoulders leading into arms, no sleeves, no elbows, no wrists attached to the body. Exactly two small floating glove-like hands exist independently, with conspicuous open background gaps between each hand and the torso. A floating hand may grip an object. Keep body, head and legs. Show this detached-hand anatomy consistently in the large hero and every small pose.
Visual style: flat, bold, simple procedural-looking cut-paper ink shapes, subtle risograph grain and screened overprint, tiny registration drift. Absolutely no black contour outlines, no drawn strokes, no 3D realism, no chrome, no painted shading. Dark midnight indigo, federal-blue clothing, cream paper highlights, warm golden eyes. HEX is burgundy, ASTRAL teal, AWARENESS orange. Dash-ready light is fluorescent pink; spawn-lantern light is warm cream and amber; health is solid pale cream beads. Keep these three signals visually distinct.
State design: base health is THREE LARGE BEADS integrated into the character, clear filled cream circles/lozenges when healthy. Lost-health beads remain visibly present as dark indigo recessed shapes, NOT white outlines. Three positions must always remain distinct and countable. Show 3/3 health with three cream beads; 1/3 with ONE cream bead and TWO dark recessed beads. Additional health capacity could add beads later, but depict just three here. Spawn-lantern protection is an independent big flame shape: warm paper-white with an amber center when active; a dark burned wick with NO light/halo when inactive. It is a status motif, not a second carried spell. In the 1/3 protected example the flame MUST remain bright; in 1/3 unprotected the same flame site MUST be dark. Never confuse lantern status with spell identity or pink dash readiness.
Composition: rebuild the comparison layout as a clean landscape 3:2 sheet with a large full-body hero at left, approximately 35% width, facing right. Upper right, THREE clearly separated SPELL VARIANTS showing the changing spell silhouette, labels HEX, ASTRAL, AWARENESS. Next row at right, THREE upper-body/gear views labeled exactly "3/3 - PROTECTED", "1/3 - PROTECTED", "1/3 - UNPROTECTED"; hold the equipped HEX spell and dash-ready state fixed across these health/lantern comparisons. Make beads and flame easy to compare. Next row, TWO small full-body poses labeled "DASH READY", "DASH SPENT"; hold 3/3 health, active lantern and HEX spell fixed, change ONLY the designated pink dash indicator to dark blue in spent. Along the bottom, a simple dark blue grotto gameplay vignette labeled "GAMEPLAY SCALE", the actual character SMALL, approximately 55-65 pixels tall, with no arms, three health beads, the warm protection flame, and large spell cue still visible. No enemies or HUD. Sparse cream uppercase labels only, no explanation paragraphs or ornate frame.
Constraints: avoid clothing clutter, tiny icons, decorative buttons, unnecessary charms, extra pouches, intricate borders. Every accessory shown must serve the requested visual identity or an explicit state cue. The masks or helmet or beret remain the dominant head silhouette. No conventional wizard pointed hat. Make the status system part of the character design, not a floating HUD.
Title (verbatim): "ARCANE COSMONAUT".
Preserve: oversized round glass helmet, dark anonymous face with two warm eyes, compact blue body, chunky boots, the gentle haunted-cosmonaut mood. Helmet glass is a screened blue filled dome and broad cream reflection crescents, not outlined.
Clothing change: MUCH SIMPLER. One plain blue sleeveless tunic/pressure-suit capsule torso, dark collar, simple blue legs and boots. Remove the long decorated tabard, patches, moon emblems, buckles, straps, belts, cuffs and ornaments. Keep just the three health beads and flame status. No arms; two disconnected small floating blue gloves.
REMOVE THE SPELL GAUNTLET COMPLETELY. No wrist or forearm devices. The spell readout needs to be much bigger and part of the silhouette.
Backpack change: replace the mechanical backpack with ONE ARCANE RELIQUARY, an ink-dark pear-shaped ceremonial vessel resting behind the torso, with one broad cream crescent opening/binding. It feels like a haunted vessel or portable shrine, an occult artifact made from simple shapes. NO hoses, no tanks, no cylindrical bottles, no mechanical dials, no gauges, no rivets, no panels, no antennae, no technological hardware.
Spell indicator: from the top of this arcane backpack emerges ONE LARGE hovering spell manifestation above the far shoulder, approximately half to two-thirds of the helmet diameter, unmistakable at gameplay size. HEX: a bold burgundy comet/spark. ASTRAL: a teal flowing ghost/shroud. AWARENESS: an orange almond-eye with a paper-white pupil. This large manifestation and the pack opening change with the spell. Do not hide it behind the helmet. Upper-right variants show the same simple head/torso/backpack, with the LARGE different spirit shapes plainly visible.
Dash cue: one small pink comet-like spirit wisp tied to the back of the helmet collar, near the other shoulder, distinct from the larger spell spirit. Ready: pink paper-bright flare. Spent: a dark blue folded wisp with no halo. No technological thruster.
Health placement: three bold cream beads in a horizontal row across the high chest. They are a clear status strip, the only marks on the simple tunic.
Lantern placement: one large warm flame in a simple dark chest medallion BELOW the health beads. Active bright flame, unprotected burned dark wick. No extra carried lantern, no mechanical chest controls.
Main hero has HEX equipped, 3/3 health, lantern protected, dash ready. Show enough open negative space to prove that both hands float independently. The glove in front of the character should look relaxed and magical, not armored.
```
